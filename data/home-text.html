On this website, I am showcasing all of the artworks that I have created so far in my creative journey, from digital artworks and animations, to physical mediums both 2D and 3D, to edible sweets decorations, and so on!

I've always loved creating ever since I was a kid, and also enjoy the exploration in trying out new techniques and mediums. Thus, I have spent many thousands of hours of my free time throughout the years into learning and experimenting in all kinds of art forms, and have built up skills in improving my abilities to create better art. It is easy to lose sight of your progress when you are only focusing on improving, so I made this website to have a way to take a step back and see how much I have improved throughout my journey.

I themed the design of this website around a memory that a lot of people may also share — my very first artwork, the stereotypical blue-sky green-grass crayon drawing that many kids love to draw at a young age. Although it wasn't very good, at least for me it is a memory that I remember quite well, and I consider it the start of my art journey. This drawing represents the beginning of my creativity and curiosity, and also the ideal world that I envisioned as a child who had yet to experience reality, the world that I still am aiming to capture within my artworks. Being such a small but momentous event in my art life, I wanted to make it an important component of my portfolio.

The artworks I have included within this website are ordered by date, with the most recent artworks appearing at the top. They are also grouped by series if the artwork was created in conjunction with other works, and sections which are roughly based around the mediums used to create the artwork. Using the links at the top of the page, you can also see a list of all series and artworks in my portfolio, also with the option to search and filter the works.

Scroll through the site to explore the works I have created, and see creativity continue to evolve!
